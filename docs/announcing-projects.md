# Announcing work in progress

For projects that have been announced (by the client, the architect or the press) but that TDA
has no photographs of yet.

## Before it goes on the site

An announcement by someone else does not mean TDA may say it lights the building. Clients often
keep their consultants confidential until opening.

1. Get approval to name TDA on the project: from the owner, the architect, or both, as the
   contract requires.
2. In the project, set **Project status** to **In progress** and fill in what is known:
   location, design architect, category, and the expected completion year.
3. In the sidebar, tick **Approved to announce** and say who approved it and when. This stays
   internal.
4. Publish.

Until step 3, the project stays off the site even when published: off the portfolio, the map,
search engines' sitemap and its own address. "Still needed" shows **Approval to announce**
meanwhile.

## Where it shows

- **Portfolio → List**: every project as text, with the place, architect, category and year.
  Work in progress shows its expected completion year.
- **Portfolio → Map**, once its location has coordinates.
- **Portfolio → On the Boards**.
- **Its own page**: the facts, with no photo. Add the announcement under **Press** (the client's
  or architect's press release, or the article) so the page links to it.

It is left out of the **Grid**, which is photographs only, and of the **Timeline** until it is
completed. Any project can also be kept off the Timeline with **Hide from the Timeline**.

## When it is built

Add the photos and set the status to **Completed**. It moves into the grid and onto the Timeline
by itself. If it is cancelled, set **Cancelled**: it leaves the Timeline and On the Boards.

Renderings from the architect can be used with their permission: upload them like photos, with
a **Credit override** such as "Rendering courtesy of Snøhetta".
